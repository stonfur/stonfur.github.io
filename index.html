About Me

Ding Shi (Member, IEEE) received the B.E. degree in Information Engineering and the Ph.D. degree in Information and Communication Engineering from Southeast University, Nanjing, China, in 2018 and 2024, respectively. From November 2022 to November 2023, he was a visiting Ph.D. student with the Division of Information Science and Engineering, KTH Royal Institute of Technology, Stockholm, Sweden.

He is currently a Postdoctoral Researcher with the Pervasive Communication Research Center, Purple Mountain Laboratories, Nanjing, China.

His research interests lie in the general areas of massive MIMO communications and signal processing for wireless communications, with current emphasis on HF skywave and LEO satellite communications. In particular, he is interested in exploiting the sparse and structured characteristics of wireless channels for efficient channel acquisition and transceiver design. He was selected for the Young Elite Scientists Sponsorship Program by the China Institute of Communications and the Jiangsu Funding Program for Excellent Postdoctoral Talent (Class A).

石丁，IEEE Member，现为紫金山实验室普适通信研究中心博士后研究员，博士后合作导师为尤肖虎院士。2018 年毕业于东南大学吴健雄学院少年班，获信息工程专业学士学位；2024 年毕业于东南大学信息科学与工程学院，获信息与通信工程专业博士学位，博士期间师从高西奇教授。2022 年 11 月至 2023 年 11 月受国家留学基金委资助，赴瑞典皇家理工学院（KTH Royal Institute of Technology）开展联合培养博士研究。

主要研究方向包括大规模 MIMO 通信、天波通信、低轨卫星通信以及无线通信信号处理。近年来主要围绕大规模 MIMO 系统中的信道结构特性及其高效利用开展研究，重点关注信道建模与信息获取、信号检测和预编码传输等问题。博士阶段围绕天波大规模 MIMO 通信开展了较为系统的研究，构建了波束结构传输的相关理论与方法，目前进一步关注天波和低轨卫星等远距离无线通信场景下的大规模 MIMO 传输理论与关键技术。

已发表 SCI 检索期刊论文 16 篇，其中第一/通讯作者论文 12 篇，发表 EI 检索会议论文 11 篇；获授权国家发明专利 15 项，美国专利 1 项。相关研究成果发表于 IEEE Transactions on Wireless Communications、IEEE Transactions on Communications 等国际期刊。入选中国通信学会青年人才托举工程和江苏省卓越博士后计划 A 档。
